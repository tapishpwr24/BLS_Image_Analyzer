    Your data, your storage

    Where records are saved

    Rating on your own?

    Nothing to set up. Your ratings and photos stay on this phone. To share them, use Records → Export CSV for a spreadsheet, or Back up below for everything including photos. Joining a team is optional.

    Send ratings and photos to Google Sheets

    Your own Sheet, or the team leader's: rows in the Sheet, photos in Google Drive. A guided setup takes about 5 minutes, once, on a computer.

    Every record is saved on this phone first. Choose where copies go when you're online. The app has no server of its own: data goes straight from this phone to the storage you choose.

    Defaults

    Rating mode

    Every leaf is rated by eye first. The app shows its score only after the rating is locked.

    Some photos of a set come back later, unannounced, to measure how consistently each person rates. Photos taken with the camera are never repeated.

    Each record keeps the rater ID, the rater's 1–9 score and %, how long the rating took, and the app's score, so rater and app can be compared later. This setting is shared with your team through the setup link.

    Scoring rules

    Applies to new photos. Each record stores which rules were used.

    Plot list

    Load your field book as a CSV with a plot column, and optionally genotype, rep and trial. Plot IDs then autocomplete, genotype and rep fill in by themselves, and Records shows which plots are still to do.

    Backup and restore

    One ZIP file with every record, photo and overlay, plus the CSV. Keep it on Drive or a computer, or restore it on another phone.

    Privacy

    Once a day the app tells its developer that it is in use: a random number for this device, the app version, phone type, installed or browser, language and continent. Never your name, ratings, photos or location. It helps us know how many people use the app.

    Read the privacy notice

    Set up a team (optional, for organisers)

    Only needed when several people send ratings to one shared Google Sheet. Team members scan this code with their phone camera to open the app with the same storage, trial and settings. Keys are included, so only share it with your team.

    This phone

    Scoring method

    Engine v. The leaf is separated from the background automatically: by colour on a black board or cloth (several leaves at once), or by the on-phone AI leaf finder in field photos. Leaf pixels are then classified as healthy, chlorotic or necrotic, and the diseased share sets the 1–9 score. Photos are analysed at up to 2048 px on the long side.

    Step 1 of 5

    Do this once, on a computer

    Ratings and photos will go to the Google account you use here. In a team, the team leader does this; members then just scan a QR code. Working alone? Use your own account.

    You need a Google account. Google's script editor does not work in the phone apps, which is why a computer is needed for this one step.

    Create a Google Sheet

    Open a new, empty Google Sheet and give it a name, for example BLS ratings 2026.

    Open a new Google Sheet

    Ratings will arrive in a tab called BLS_results, the people using the app in a tab called Users, and photos in a Drive folder called BLS Leaf Scorer photos.

    Paste the script

    1. In the Sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script.
    2. Delete everything in the editor.
    3. Tap Copy script below and paste it into the editor.
    4. Click the Save icon.

    Your team key, , is already inside the script. It stops strangers from writing to your Sheet. Nothing needs editing.

    Copy button not working?

    Publish it

    1. Click Deploy → New deployment.
    2. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
    3. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
    4. Click Authorize access and choose your account. If Google says it hasn't verified the app, click Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe), then Allow. This is your own script in your own account.
    5. Copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec.

    Connect the app

    Paste the Web app URL, then test the connection.

    Connected

    New ratings, photos and thumbnails now go to your Google Sheet when this device is online.

    Working as a team? Under Set up a team, show the QR code. Members scan it and tap Join team, and their ratings go to this Sheet too.

    Photograph or pick a leaf

    Take photo for a leaf in front of you, Gallery for one saved photo, or Rate a set of photos to go through many, one at a time.

    One leaf, filling the frame, in shade. A black board behind it works best.

    Rate it by eye first

    The app hides its score until you have rated. Check the plant ID (use − / + or scan the label), tap your 1–9 score on the band, then tap the closest % diseased.

    Judge the leaf, not the score you expect. Other leaves, a hand or labels in the photo? Tap Mark around the leaf and draw a loop: the app then looks only inside it.

Privacy notice

BLS Leaf Scorer, developed by Tapish Pawar, Ameen Lab, Plant Pathology. Last updated 27 November 2026.

The short version

  • Your ratings, photos and name stay on your device unless you connect a Google Sheet or share a file.
  • The app counts devices anonymously, once a day, so we know how many people use it. You can switch this off.
  • There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What stays on your device

Ratings, photos, the name or rater ID you enter, and your settings are saved in the app's storage on your phone or computer. Deleting the app's site data, or uninstalling it, removes them.

What goes where you choose

If you, or your team leader, connect a Google Sheet, your ratings, photos and name are sent straight from your device to that Google account. The app's developer has no access to it. If you export a spreadsheet or a backup, it goes wherever you send it.

Never sent: your name or rater ID, ratings, photos, plant IDs, GPS location, contacts, or anything else on your device.

This lets us report how many devices use the app, how many are active, and on which kinds of device, without knowing who anyone is.

Switching the count off

In the app, open Settings → Privacy and untick Count this device anonymously. Nothing more is sent from that device.

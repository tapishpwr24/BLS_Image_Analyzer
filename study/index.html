    Score a leaf on the Analyze tab and tap Save record. Records stay on this phone and sync when you're online.

    Your data, your storage

    Where records are saved

    Every record is saved on this phone first. Choose where copies go when you're online. The app has no server of its own: data goes straight from this phone to the storage you choose.

    Defaults

    Study mode (rater evaluation)

    Each record keeps the rater ID, the rater's 1–9 score and %, how long the rating took, and the app's score, so rater and app can be compared later. These settings are shared with your team through the setup link.

    Scoring rules

    Applies to new photos. Each record stores which rules were used. Taught colours are shared with your team through the setup link.

    Plot list

    Load your field book as a CSV with a plot column, and optionally genotype, rep and trial. Plot IDs then autocomplete, genotype and rep fill in by themselves, and Records shows which plots are still to do.

    Backup and restore

    One ZIP file with every record, photo and overlay, plus the CSV. Keep it on Drive or a computer, or restore it on another phone.

    Set up a team

    Team members scan this code with their phone camera to open the app with the same storage, trial and settings. Keys are included, so only share it with your team.

    This phone

    Scoring method

    Engine v. The leaf is separated from the background automatically: by colour on a black board or cloth (several leaves at once), or by the on-phone AI leaf finder in field photos. Leaf pixels are then classified as healthy, chlorotic or necrotic, and the diseased share sets the 1–9 score. Photos are analysed at up to 2048 px on the long side.

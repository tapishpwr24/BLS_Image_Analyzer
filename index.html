BLS Leaf Scorer

Rate bacterial leaf streak on wheat leaves by eye, then see how the app scores the same leaf from the photo. Free, works offline, on phones and computers.

Open the app

Scan with your phone camera

How it works

  1. Photograph or pick a leafTake a photo, choose one from the gallery, or rate a whole set of photos one at a time.
  2. Rate it by eye firstTap the plant ID, a 1–9 BLS score and the % diseased. The app's score stays hidden until you lock your rating.
  3. Compare and saveThe app finds the leaf, scores it, and shows its result next to yours. Everything is saved with your name and the plant ID.

Install it like an app

Android

Open the app in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone and iPad

Open the app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar.

On your own or as a team

On your own

Nothing to set up. Ratings and photos stay on your device; export a spreadsheet or a backup whenever you need them.

As a team

The team leader connects a Google Sheet once (Settings → Connect a Google Sheet) and shares the team QR code. Everyone's ratings and photos then go to the leader's Sheet and Drive.
